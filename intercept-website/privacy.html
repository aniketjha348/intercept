Privacy Promise

Security should never come at the cost of personal privacy. Here is how Intercept AI treats your data.

  • Your OTPs, passwords and card details are never stored. They are analyzed entirely in volatile memory to protect you in real-time, and scrubbed instantly from anything saved or logged.
  • We remember attack patterns, not your secrets. Scam DNA maps abstract malicious tactics (e.g. imposter bank authority + artificial countdown + OTP demand) — never your personal messages or private conversations — and stays scoped to your device.
  • Zero ads, zero data monetization. Your calls, messages, and security incidents are never sold, auctioned, or used to build marketing profiles.
  • You remain in complete control. You can take over any active call with one tap, stop screening at any moment, or purge your device history at any time.
Built in India for Indian families facing UPI & OTP fraud. Aligned by design with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
