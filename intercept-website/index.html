The Threat Landscape

Why unknown calls in India have become dangerous.

Organized scam syndicates in Jamtara, Mewat, and Cambodia no longer just pitch simple lottery prizes. They use psychological coercion, legal intimidation, and urgent deadlines.

1. Digital Arrest Coercion

Callers pose as Mumbai Police, CBI officers, or Customs officials. They claim an illegal courier containing drugs was booked in your name, present fabricated warrants, and demand immediate video compliance and money transfers.

2. The 2-Hour Bill Deadline

Urgent automated SMS and follow-up calls claiming your electricity connection will be severed at 9:30 PM tonight, or your SBI account frozen within 2 hours. Victims panic and call back fake customer service numbers.

3. OTP & Remote APK Traps

The final step of every telecom scam is extracting credentials. Scammers ask victims to share an incoming OTP or install rogue remote access APKs (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) to drain bank accounts.

Handset Architecture

How Intercept handles an incoming call.

01

Local address book check

When a call arrives, Intercept immediately checks your contacts on the handset. If it's a family member, friend, or saved business contact, your phone rings normally. Only unsaved, unknown numbers are routed to the screening engine.

02

Autonomous voice screening

Intercept answers the call quietly on your behalf. The AI introduces itself and asks the caller to state their identity, employer, and purpose. It speaks fluent Hindi and English, adapting to the caller's language.

03

Real-time speech evaluation

As the caller speaks, speech is transcribed and evaluated in volatile memory. The Scam DNA™ engine checks for coercive phrases, institutional authority claims (Police, CBI, SBI, Electricity Board), and artificial deadlines.

04

Immediate severance or pass-through

If the caller demands an OTP, password, UPI MPIN, or asks you to install an APK, the line is severed immediately. If it's a legitimate caller (like a delivery driver), your phone rings so you can take over.

Device Transparency

Every permission requested, and why it is needed.

Android requires explicit permissions for call screening apps. Here is the exact reason for each permission.

android.permission.MANAGE_OWN_CALLS

Default Phone App

Needed to answer unknown calls on your behalf, route caller speech, and terminate the call automatically if an OTP demand is made.

android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO

Microphone Access

Needed to transcribe caller audio in volatile memory. Audio is processed on the fly and discarded immediately after the call ends.

android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER

Notification Access

Needed to inspect incoming SMS headers for fake bank IDs and phishing URLs. Notification text from muted or private apps is never inspected.

android.permission.READ_CONTACTS (Local Only)

Contacts Check

Used strictly on your device to let your saved contacts ring through normally. Your address book is never uploaded, shared, or indexed.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will it answer calls from my family and contacts?

No. Your saved phone contacts always ring through normally without delay or screening. Only unknown or unsaved numbers are intercepted by the AI guardian.

Does Intercept AI read my private WhatsApp messages?

No. Intercept AI only scans incoming notification text previews as they appear on your phone. Private chats and encrypted message contents are never opened or accessed.

Where do my OTPs and passwords go?

Nowhere. Intercept AI analyzes text in volatile memory to protect you in real-time. OTPs, CVVs, and banking PINs are scrubbed instantly and are never logged or stored anywhere.

Does it work without active internet?

Yes. The app contains an embedded on-device heuristic engine that continues detecting urgent OTP demands and high-pressure threats even in airplane mode or during poor network coverage.

Which Android phones are supported?

Any smartphone running Android 10 and above (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, Google Pixel, Motorola). On Xiaomi and Vivo devices, enabling autostart ensures round-the-clock defense.
