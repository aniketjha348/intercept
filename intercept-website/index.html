Asked often

Will it answer my mom's calls too?

No. Saved contacts always ring through normally. Only unknown numbers get screened.

Does it read my WhatsApp chats?

No. It can only scan message notifications as they arrive — muted chats stay private, and chats themselves are never opened.

Where do my OTPs go?

Nowhere. They are checked in memory and scrubbed from anything saved. We store attack patterns, never secrets.

Does it work without internet?

The on-device rules keep working, but live screening and reports need the backend. Point the app at a reachable server URL.

Which phones work?

Android 10 and above. Xiaomi, Vivo and Oppo owners should also allow autostart so protection survives the night.
