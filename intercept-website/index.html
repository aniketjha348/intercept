Frequently asked questions

Will it answer calls from my family and contacts?

No. Your saved phone contacts always ring through normally without delay or screening. Only unknown or suspicious numbers are intercepted by the AI guardian.

Does Intercept AI read my private WhatsApp messages?

No. Intercept AI only scans incoming notification text previews as they appear on your phone. Private chats and encrypted message contents are never opened or accessed.

Where do my OTPs and passwords go?

Nowhere. Intercept AI analyzes text in volatile memory to protect you in real-time. OTPs, CVVs, and banking PINs are scrubbed instantly and are never logged or stored anywhere.

Does it work without active internet?

Yes. The app contains an embedded on-device heuristic engine that continues detecting urgent OTP demands and high-pressure threats even in airplane mode or during poor network coverage.

Which Android phones are supported?

Any smartphone running Android 10 and above (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, Google Pixel, Motorola). On Xiaomi and Vivo devices, enabling autostart ensures round-the-clock defense.
